Whether you are analyzing earthquake/flood vulnerability in existing neighborhoods or drawing a brand-new urban master plan on vacant reserve land, this plugin turns complex GIS layers into ready-to-use planning zones and executive reports in seconds: (1) 9 One-Click Zoning Strategy Presets + Full Manual Control: Choose ready-made planning presets — Integrated Planning & Vulnerability Synthesis, Vacant Land & New Development Potential (separating unbuilt high-vacant-land blocks from dense built-up fabric), Facility Accessibility, Agricultural Land & Rural Fringe Protection, Commercial Core & Mixed-Use Corridors, Industrial Cohesion & Transition Interfaces (keeping industrial blocks unified while detecting Industry-Residential and Industry-Agriculture interfaces), Morphological / Natural Valley Corridor Orientation, or Seismic / Flood Vulnerability — or fine-tune all 11 weight parameters manually with synchronized sliders and dynamic impact badges.
