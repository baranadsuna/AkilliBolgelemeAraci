Akıllı Bölgeleme Aracı — Urban Zoning & Vulnerability Analysis Tool

Academic methodology and operational guide for spatial contiguity-constrained Ward clustering, advanced boundary compactness, macro-zone area cap exemptions, calibrated residential block population distribution, and 3D disaster vulnerability synthesis.

1. Mathematical Formulation & Algorithmic Pipeline

1.1 Street-Block Morphological Cellularization & Sliver Filtering (analiz.py :: build_cells_from_roads)

To resolve the Modifiable Areal Unit Problem (MAUP) without arbitrary grids, study boundaries and road network lines are topologically noded via QgsGeometry.unaryUnion and polygonized into street-bounded urban cells. Road medians and sliver polygons lacking real buildings are detected via Polsby-Popper compactness $PP$ and morphological thickness $T$:

$$PP(c) = \frac{4\pi A_c}{P_c^2}, \qquad T(c) = \frac{2 A_c}{P_c}$$

Cells without building blocks satisfying $A_c < 450\text{ m}^2$, $PP(c) < 0.10$, or $T(c) < 14.0\text{ m}$ (or collapsing under inward morphological erosion of $-7.0\text{ m}$) are automatically absorbed into the adjacent urban block sharing the longest boundary edge.

1.2 Contiguity-Constrained Ward Agglomeration with Shape Compactness (algoritma.py :: ward_merge)

Following Ward (1963) (DOI: 10.1080/01621459.1963.10500845), agglomeration merges contiguous clusters $(A, B) \in E_{\text{adj}}$ minimizing error sum of squares ($\Delta \text{ESS}$). In v1.8.0, the cost formulation incorporates boundary shape compactness $\Phi_{\text{geo}}$, macro-zone cohesion, and dynamic area-cap factor $\kappa(A, B)$:

$$\Delta \text{ESS}(A, B) = \frac{W_A W_B}{W_A + W_B}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{D} (\bar{z}_{A,k} - \bar{z}_{B,k})^2 + \varepsilon\right) \cdot \Phi_{\text{affinity}}(A, B) \cdot \left(1 + \kappa(A, B) \left(\frac{\text{Area}_A + \text{Area}_B}{C_{\text{area}}}\right)^p\right)$$

where:

  • Macro-Zone Area Cap Exemption: When clusters $A$ and $B$ are both industrial ($\text{san} \ge 0.35$) or agricultural ($\text{tar} \ge 0.60$), $\kappa(A, B) = 0.0$ and $\Phi_{\text{affinity}} = 0.04$, allowing massive industrial estates (OIZ) and agricultural plains to remain unified into large coherent blocks without artificial splitting.
  • Industrial-Residential Strict Isolation: When industry borders residential fabric ($\text{san}_A \ge 0.30$ and $\text{kon}_B \ge 0.20$), $\Phi_{\text{affinity}} = 45.0$, strictly preventing industry from encroaching into residential zones.
  • Facility Deficit Scaling: In dense residential zones with severe facility shortages ($\text{donati} < 6\%$), $\kappa(A, B) = 2.4$, keeping neighborhood zones smaller and focused so planning deficits are visibly highlighted.
  • Boundary Elongation & Throat Penalty: If the shared boundary $L_{\text{shared}} < 10\text{ m}$, cost increases by $(1 + 3.5 \cdot w_{\text{geo}})$, preventing thin winding road corridor tails (such as narrow north-south strips).

1.3 3D Disaster Vulnerability Framework (analiz.py :: compute_vulnerability)

Grounded in the sustainability science framework of Turner et al. (2003) (DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1231335100), seismic and flood vulnerability combine Exposure ($E$), physical Sensitivity ($S$), and Adaptive Capacity ($AC$):

$$E = 0.6\,e_{\text{pop\_dens}} + 0.4\,e_{\text{bldg\_count}}, \qquad V_{\text{seismic}} = \frac{E \times S_{\text{seismic}}}{\max(1.0,\, AC_{\text{seismic}})}, \qquad V_{\text{flood}} = \frac{E \times S_{\text{flood}}}{\max(1.0,\, AC_{\text{flood}})}$$ $$V_{\text{total}} = 0.6\,V_{\text{seismic}} + 0.4\,V_{\text{flood}}$$

2. Six Methodological Invariants (Zero-Distortion Urban Rules)

Invariant 1: Industrial & Agricultural Zero-Population Rule

Industrial/storage ($\text{sanayi} \ge 0.45$) and agricultural/cemetery zones receive a strict resident population of $0$. Total population is distributed exclusively across residential and mixed commercial-residential blocks weighted by building count, parcel area, and story height.

Invariant 2: TAKS < 5% Open-Space Order Safeguard

Sub-zones exhibiting building coverage $\text{TAKS} < 5\%$ or open/agricultural ratio $> 70\%$ are never mislabeled as Attached Order; they are strictly classified as "Yapısız / Açık Alan" or "Seyrek Kırsal Doku".

Invariant 3: Macro-Zone Cohesion ("Büyük Alanlar Baba Olsun")

Organized Industrial Zones (OIZ) and wide fertile agricultural plains are exempt from arbitrary cluster size ceilings, preserving their continuous macro-scale spatial integrity while keeping urban residential neighborhoods appropriately subdivided.

Invariant 4: Geometric Compactness & Tail Elimination

Agglomeration strictly penalizes narrow throat junctions ($L_{\text{shared}} < 10\text{ m}$) and high bounding aspect ratios, eradicating long winding corridor tails and ensuring all planning zones have cohesive, well-proportioned polygons.

Invariant 5: Decoupled Natural Barriers

Social and technical facilities (schools, healthcare centers, public parks) are decoupled from the Natural Thresholds table, ensuring natural barriers (faults, streams, floodplains, DEM slope) function purely as physical/environmental constraints.

Invariant 6: Top-3 $|\Delta Z|$ Dynamic Boundary Justification

Each sub-zone's boundary rationale dynamically ranks the top 3 non-coordinate variables with the largest absolute standardized difference $|\bar{Z}_{A,k} - \bar{Z}_{B,k}|$ against adjacent zones, providing transparent and verifiable spatial explanations.
